Context-Aware Cross-Slice U-Net

Glioma segmentation on BraTS 2020. A shared 2-D U-Net encodes each of 5 adjacent slices; a Cross-Slice Transformer fuses them at the bottleneck; a U-Net decoder reconstructs the centre slice. Every value is taken directly from cst/model.py and cst/config.py.

4.33 M params · baseline U-Net (K=1) 6.70 M params · full model (K=5) width base=48 in B×5×4×240×240 out B×3×240×240
U-Net encoder / decoder (convolutional) Cross-Slice Transformer — novel module skip connection (centre slice)

Read it as a “U”: down the left arm (encoder, 240→30), across the bottom (cross-slice fusion), up the right arm (decoder, 30→240). Dashed lines are the centre slice’s skip connections; K=1 removes the orange block, leaving a plain U-Net (the baseline).
